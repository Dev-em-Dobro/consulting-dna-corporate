note is the record of what moved, why it looks the way it does, and the short list of what is
still waiting on your side.

## 1. Home — the hero and the awards

ring so the row stays aligned. Send the logo files and they become logos, with no other change.

**"See the work" now goes to Clients & Impact.** It was pointing at Our Approach, which had been
flagged internally as a mismatch between the label and the destination. Your email settled it.

## 2. Home — the numbers, the logos and the people

- **The four figures sit in one horizontal line.** They were a 2x2 block before, inside a fixed
  760px width. That width cap had to go, or the labels would not fit across four columns.
- **The client marks are on the impact cards** — HEINEKEN, Coca-Cola and Shell. They sit on a
  white chip, because those files are coloured marks on transparency and disappear against the
  dark card.
- **The Our People headings are in red.**

**On the photographs.** You said the quality was not great and that some were cropped. Both were
true, and one of them had a cause we could fix outright: **two of the pictures in the carousel
were the same photograph**, the Singapore courtyard group, the second time in a smaller copy
that was being stretched. That duplicate is gone.

Six more were re-framed, one by one. The worst was a picture where the automatic centre crop cut
the head off the facilitator who was standing.

**Two pictures still need you.** One is 768x1024 and another 739x1131 — both portraits sitting
in a landscape frame, so only about 40% of each is ever visible, and both are below the
resolution the slot asks for. There is no larger version anywhere in what we have. If the
originals exist, send them; if not, our recommendation is to drop those two.

**Three more are worth your eye**, and these are content calls, not technical ones: one is a 2x2
collage with "On Stage, Always" and a clipped copyright line burned into it, three carry YPO
event branding burned into the frame, and one is mostly the backs of people's heads.

**Two places where we knowingly differ from the drawing:**

  sampled pixel by pixel all three measure the same tone.

drawings, and the second one was in the folder all along under a different name. It frames the
page from the cards down, so it shows the practices and the case study line but not the "How we

That block stays. Its headline and paragraph are the ones from your first drawing, unchanged.
