The notification is SENT from a domain verified in Resend. corporatednaconsulting.com

DECIDED 11 SEPTEMBER: do not touch the zone now. Do it as part of the cutover, when
we are in Cloudflare anyway.

Why it is safe to do then, verified by DNS query on 11 September:
  - The zone IS on Cloudflare (pam/mario.ns.cloudflare.com) and Dev em Dobro has had
    access since the invitation was accepted on 13 August. Axon is not involved.
  - resend._domainkey and send.corporatednaconsulting.com are both FREE - no TXT, no
    MX. Nothing to overwrite.
  - Resend puts its SPF on the `send` SUBDOMAIN, not the apex. THE ROOT SPF IS NOT
    EDITED, which matters here: the apex TXT already carries five includes plus an ip4
    block (SendGrid, Zoho, Outlook, Mailchimp, dnssmarthost) and is near SPF's ten
    DNS-lookup ceiling. Adding to it could break mail that works today.
  - The apex MX (Microsoft 365) is untouched. An MX on `send` does not compete with it.
  - DMARC is v=DMARC1; p=none - no enforcement, so nothing can start bouncing. rua
    reports go to Carol.Medcalf@corporatednaconsulting.com.

What the change is, when the time comes:
  a. Create corporatednaconsulting.com in the Resend account that owns the production
     RESEND_API_KEY. THIS ACCOUNT IS NOT YET DECIDED - see below.
  b. Add in Cloudflare, DNS-only (not proxied): TXT resend._domainkey (value from
     Resend), MX send -> feedback-smtp.<region>.amazonses.com priority 10, TXT send ->
     v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~all. Skip the tracking CNAME.
  c. Set LEAD_NOTIFY_FROM on the CMS project in Vercel to an address on the domain, and
     LEAD_NOTIFY_TO to ceo-office@corporatednaconsulting.com. Redeploy the CMS.

OPEN QUESTION, AND IT IS THE SUPABASE QUESTION AGAIN: which Resend account owns this?
