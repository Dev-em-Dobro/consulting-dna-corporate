Hi G and Team,
I’ve been looking closely at our new site alongside Explore Performance, which I really like, and I think sometimes the comparison helps clarify or express in words what I see as  currently missing from ours. Pl see this as construvtive feedback with no blame but over the last two weeks the website direction isnt sitting right with me and I dont feel the inner peace when you know its right.
Our site is clean and has strong content, but at the moment it feels too corporate, dull/distant and digitally generated. Therea re some good elements which I will point out whenw e talk. Explore Performance feels much more human, warm, personable, authentic, fresh and alive. It draws me in. You get a sense of the people and the experience behind the business almost immediately, and there is almost a cinematic quality to the site that keeps my attention as I move through it.
So I kept asking myself... what is creating that difference, and what are the tangible elements we can incorporate into CDNA without simply copying them?
For me, it comes down to the use of real photography and people, a broader and more natural use of colour, stronger contrast between sections, fewer words on screen, bigger and more confident typography, more whitespace, varied page layouts, strong client proof and a much more editorial approach to storytelling.
Importantly, their colour palette doesn't feel artificially limited. Their core brand colours are there, but the photography introduces blues, greens, warm neutrals, skin tones and different environments. The brand holds it together without every page needing to look the same. I think we can learn from that. Our red should be a recognisable CDNA signature, but not a constraint.
The biggest difference for me is that Explore Performance shows you its world, whereas we currently explain ours. I want us to shift that balance... less explaining and more experiencing CDNA.

A few things I’d like us to incorporate as we develop ours:
More real photography and real people. Our leadership portraits are strong, but beyond these we need much more real imagery of us working... facilitating, speaking, with clients, in conversations, travelling, laughing, thinking, challenging. We are Candid as well as polished. I want to avoid generic stock imagery and anything that feels obviously AI generated. The current brain/world-map imagery, for example, makes us feel more like a generic financial services/corporate or technology consultancy than CDNA.Even our Meet the LT feels stiff people. We are not.
More visual storytelling and less text. Explore Performance gives one idea room to land... a strong headline, a powerful photograph, a short piece of copy, then the next idea. Some of our pages currently feel like a lot of information presented at once. The 5H page is a good example... the thinking is strong, but visually it becomes four paragraphs to read. We need to edit much harder for the web.
More warmth and variation in colour. I particularly dislike we are restricted to one or two brand colours. There is a coherent underlying palette, but a good website's photography brings blues, greens, sea, sky, skin tones, warm neutrals etc. It feels rich and natural. Our red should remain a CDNA signature, but it doesn’t need to dominate every page. I’d like red to become more of an accent, with photography and a broader range of complementary tones creating warmth and visual interest.
More editorial typography. Their combination of strong contemporary headlines with more expressive type for quotes and storytelling creates personality. Ours currently feels quite uniform and corporate. Could we explore more typographic contrast while staying sophisticated and premium?
Fewer boxes and cards. The IP/Diagnostics section currently feels a little like a software/product site. Our IP is one of our greatest differentiators, so I’d rather bring it alive through beautiful visualisation, photography, provocative statements and evidence of it being used than simply putting frameworks into boxes. Our 5H is not talking to me at all. Feels theory and distant /classroom style.
Right use of whitespace and stronger page rhythm. Rather than repeating headline + paragraph + cards, I’d like the pages to have a more varied rhythm... large image... bold statement... whitespace... story... framework... client proof... quote... image... impact numbers. Scrolling should feel like discovering CDNA rather than reading a corporate brochure.
Bring our client proof to life. Explore Performance does this particularly well. Their UBS example quickly tells you the challenge, what they did and the scale of the impact, with big numbers alongside it. We have exceptional client stories and global scale, so let's make those visually impossible to miss rather than burying them in copy.
Make our people feel like personalities, not an organisational chart. The portraits are good, but I’d love the site to reveal more of who we actually are... candid images, short perspectives, quotes, perhaps video. I want a CHRO or CEO looking at the site to think, “I want these people in the room with my leaders.”
The overall shift I’m looking for is:
Less corporate consultancy... more human.
Less explaining... more showing.
Less generic/AI imagery... more real CDNA.
Less boxy -ness ... more visual richness and personality.
More client and human evidence... less marketing /explaining language.
CDNA has its own very distinctive story.  The website needs to make people feel all of that.
A useful test for every page might be: if we removed the CDNA logo, could this page belong to almost any leadership consultancy? If the answer is yes, we haven't yet brought enough of CDNA into it.
I’d love us to use Explore Performance as a benchmark for the humanity, visual confidence, colour, photography, simplicity and authenticity we are aiming for... while creating something unmistakably ours.
Thanks,
Rhea
