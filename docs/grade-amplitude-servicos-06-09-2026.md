# Where we play — clients by service line

Right now the website shows **one case study per service**. That reads as depth, but it also reads as
if each service had been delivered once. What it doesn't show is the thing Ria described on the
call — that Heineken has had all of our services and GSK three, and that the awards we win are for
all of that work, not for one project.

This document collects the data for that. Two parts: the service list has to be settled first,
because it decides the columns.

---

## Part 1 — The service list, first

We can't draw the grid until we know what the services are. Three things don't line up today:

**The site currently publishes nine services.** These are live right now:

**But the brief works from a list of eight**, and the two lists are not the same one renamed. The
brief separates *Manager Development* from *High Performing Teams*, which the site still publishes
merged as a single service. The brief has no *Leadership Development* and no *Talent & Succession*;
the site has both. *Inclusion & Diversity* exists but is unpublished.

**And family business isn't anywhere** — not on the site, not in the brief's eight.

So, three questions:

1. **How many services are there, and what are they called?** Nine as published, the brief's eight,
   or a third list.
2. **Is *Manager Development* its own service, or does it stay merged into *High-Performing Teams &
   Manager Impact*?**
3. **Where does *family business* go** — a tenth service, or part of an existing one?

Everything below uses the nine currently published, because that's what the case tags in the CMS are
written against. If the list changes, the columns change with it and we re-cut the grid — the answers
you give below still hold, we just move them.

## What we do with it

One band per service line, with the client logos underneath it, in the spirit of the *Where We Play &
What We Deliver* slide. One case study per service stays exactly as it is — this sits alongside it and
answers the question your client's daughter asked after the Mongolia story: *do you have more?*

The visual treatment is Gilly's call. This document is only the data behind it.

**The one thing that would make this section impossible:** a grid with three ticks in it. Breadth is
the whole argument, so if the honest answer is that most services have been delivered to one or two
clients, tell us — the section is a bad idea and we drop it rather than publish a thin grid.

## Notas internas (não enviar)

- **Fonte do pré-preenchimento:** `docs/backup-cms-cases-2026-09-01.json`, campo `data.facets.service`
  de cada case publicado. Nada foi inferido.
- **Fonte da lista de serviços:** `docs/backup-cms-solutions-2026-09-01.json`, entradas com
  `status: published`, deduplicadas por slug. `leadership-development` aparece duas vezes com o mesmo
  título — provável duplicata a limpar no CMS, contada uma vez aqui.
- **Fonte da lista de clientes:** `lib/logos.ts`, os 27 logos curados do mural. Quatro dos oito
  clientes com case (GSK, Heineken, Aviva, Levi Strauss) **não estão nesse mural**, embora os
  arquivos existam em `public/logos`. Se a seção mostrar logos por serviço, esses quatro precisam
  entrar — e o próprio `lib/logos.ts` diz que incluir nome no mural é decisão de aprovação da CDNA,
  não mudança de código.
- **A lista de oito do briefing** está em `docs/tabela-conteudo-cdna-01-09-2026.md`, Tabela 1.
- **Coluna 2 (`Leadership & Culture Transformation`) marcada em 7 de 8** é o tipo de dado que não
  discrimina. Se voltar assim depois do preenchimento deles, a grade não prova amplitude — prova que
  a taxonomia é grossa demais, e aí o assunto vira outro.
