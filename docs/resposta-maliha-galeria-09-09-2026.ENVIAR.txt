Hi — good question, and sorry for the wait. We went and looked properly before answering rather
than guessing, which is what took the time.

On the video: we have been through the team page, the old site and your Team outline, and there
is no video on any of them. The only videos we found anywhere are five on an old CorporateDNA
YouTube channel, three of which used to be embedded on the "See us in Action" page, which no
longer opens. Is that what you had in mind? Just want to be sure we are replacing the right
thing.

On the gallery itself: send us the photographs and we will come back with a way to show the team
on the page. The right format depends on what the pictures are — portraits, candid shots from a
programme, and one wide group photograph each want something different, and choosing the format
before seeing the material usually means building it twice.

On linking through to it: rather than making the team photograph itself the link,
our suggestion would be a section at the foot of the About page with a button through to the
team. Two reasons. A large image that quietly navigates somewhere is easy to miss, and the
people who do notice cannot tell where it goes before they click. And keeping the route at the
end means a reader finishes About before they leave it — anyone who wants the team sooner
already has it in the menu.

We are working on the About page right now, so it is still moving. We will share a version for
you to review later today.
