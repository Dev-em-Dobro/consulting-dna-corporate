1.  Executive summary: our understanding & response
This is not a re-skin. Corporate DNA is a boutique global consultancy in leadership, executive coaching and executive-team transformation, with a strength (global reach, C-suite relationships, a proprietary 5H Framework, award recognition and measurable outcomes) that the current website does not communicate. Today the site reads like a broad training vendor rather than a premium boutique advisory. Our brief is to change that: to turn the website into the firm's central platform of credibility and business development.
A senior visitor, a CEO, CHRO, CLO or other decision-maker, should understand within seconds who you help, which leadership and transformation problems you solve, what makes you different, the concrete results you have delivered, the people behind the firm, and how to start a conversation.
What we heard, and how we will handle it
Prove, don't describe. The biggest visible gap is proof. We lead with impact, named outcomes, embedded video and metrics, inside the flow, not hidden on a late page.
Organise around the client, not your service list. Navigation is rebuilt around decision-maker journeys, capped to a scannable top level, with proof reachable within two clicks.
A clean, secure technical base. We do not migrate the compromised WordPress/Elementor stack. We start from a clean environment; legacy content is only migrated after review and sanitisation.
A CMS your team can actually use. Structured content types for cases, solutions, people, regions and insights; roles, versioning and staging, so routine updates no longer require a developer.
Protect focus; sequence ambition. We honour the internal tension in your BRD (ambition vs. restraint) by delivering a high-impact foundation first and sequencing AI, diagnostics and advanced features into a clearly-scoped Phase 2 with its own budget.
This document focuses only on Phase 1: Foundation / Essential: everything needed to launch a new, secure, premium English site. The later layers you requested, Recommended add-ons, Multilingual (ES/PT), and Phase 2 / Intelligence (AI, 5H diagnostic, automation), are noted where relevant and scoped separately, so you can approve and launch the foundation first without over-committing.

3.  Technology recommendation & rationale
Our recommendation: a headless architecture, a Next.js front end with a custom CMS we build for you, on Vercel with a global CDN and a Neon serverless-Postgres database. This directly answers your priorities on security, global performance, editability, multilingual readiness and AI-readiness, while avoiding the failure modes of the current stack.
A core differentiator: we build your CMS, we do not rent one. Rather than putting your content on a third-party platform (Sanity, Storyblok, WordPress and similar), Dev em Dobro develops a custom content management system tailored to exactly your content types, cases, solutions, people, regions, insights. The CMS is our own product, and we license it to you at no cost, under a licence that stays in place even if the partnership ends. There are no per-seat or per-plan fees, and nothing about your platform is dictated by another vendor's roadmap or pricing.
Why headless, concretely
Security by architecture. The public site is served as pre-rendered/static pages from a CDN, there is no live PHP/plugin database exposed to the internet on every request, which removes the exact class of vulnerability that led to the previous breaches. The editing environment is separate from what visitors touch.
Global performance. Pages are delivered from an edge network close to London, Miami, Singapore, Dubai and Riyadh, supporting fast loads on GCC/APAC mobile networks and Core Web Vitals in Google's 'Good' band.
Editable by non-technical staff. Our custom CMS gives your team structured, block-based editing with preview, built around your content, no code, no developer for routine updates.
Multilingual-ready from day one. The content model supports locales natively, so ES/PT (and later Arabic or others) are added as content, not as a rebuild.
Integration & AI-ready. Everything is API/webhook-first, so automations and a future AI assistant plug into the same content layer rather than being bolted on.
No third-party lock-in. Your site, content and data are yours, in your own accounts and database. The CMS is our own product, licensed to you at no cost, and the licence keeps working even if the partnership ends, so you are never cut off from running or editing your site.
Stack: Next.js front end · a custom CMS built by Dev em Dobro · Vercel hosting with global CDN (serves both the site and the CMS) · Neon serverless-Postgres database. Details confirmed in Discovery.
4.  Architecture decision & why not WordPress
You asked us to compare options and to explain whether WordPress could be superior. Our position is clear: for a platform with this security history and these ambitions, we recommend a custom headless architecture and we do not recommend WordPress, not the current Elementor site, and not a new WordPress build. 8.  Secure migration strategy
Given the prior breaches, we treat the existing site as untrusted and never migrate its live base.
Clean-room build. The new platform is built in a fresh, isolated environment, no reuse of the current WordPress/Elementor codebase, themes or plugins.
Inventory & triage. We inventory existing pages, media and URLs, and decide keep / rewrite / consolidate / retire with you.
Sanitise before import. Only approved content and media are migrated, after malware/integrity checks and clean re-export, no database carry-over from the old site.
Legitimate vs malicious URLs. The migration plan distinguishes legitimate legacy URLs from injected or malicious URLs (spam/gambling created during the intrusions). Legitimate URLs receive mapped 301 redirects; malicious URLs are handled through appropriate 404/410 responses and search-engine removal procedures.
Preserve SEO equity. A 301 redirect map covering the legitimate indexed legacy URLs, tested before launch, protects rankings.
Safe cutover. Launch from staging with a tested rollback plan; DNS cutover only after QA sign-off.
