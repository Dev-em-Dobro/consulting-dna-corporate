We have been working on the *About* page. It is still in development, but it is
far enough along to be worth a look:

https://consulting-dna-corporate-alpha.vercel.app/about

*Please treat this as a preview, not a review.* We are closing the page
tomorrow — that is the point to send feedback, and we would rather you spend
your time on it once, on something finished, than twice.

One thing worth explaining, because it will be the first thing you notice.

*The sections deliberately do not look alike.* About is a long page — nine
blocks — and if each one were a heading with text underneath, scrolling it
would feel like reading the same section nine times. So each block is built
differently: one is a full screen with the skyline, one is a photograph with
the quote laid over its edge, one is dark with a photograph behind it, one is
a narrow single column, one is a row of cards. The variety is doing a job, not
decorating.

*The five that are settled* — these are close to final, and worth your
attention first:

1. The opening screen
2. Keeping Leadership Real — the team photograph and your quote
3. Why Corporate DNA exists
4. What we promise
5. What we believe

*Everything after those is still moving.* Where we work, the offices and the
regions are going through another pass. Comments on them are welcome but they
will change anyway.

One thing that is not a bug: in *Keeping Leadership Real* you will see a grey
rectangle where the team photograph goes. The layout is finished — the
photograph is the one we are waiting on. It is the one already in the approved
Our Identity slide, from the CDNA Overview Deck of 22 January. If someone can
send that file across, that block is done.

*Rhea — is this the direction you had in mind?* That is the one answer we would
most like before tomorrow. The page was built from Maliha's outline, and a good
deal of what is on the screen came from our reading of it rather than from
anything anyone said directly. If the whole thing is pointing the wrong way, it
is much cheaper to hear that now than after we finish it.

*Open it on a desktop if you can.* It works on a phone, but the desktop layout
is the one we have been designing against and the one that is furthest along —
the phone version is catching up.

If a page looks the same as last time you opened it, reload holding
*Ctrl+Shift+R* — the browser keeps the old styling.
